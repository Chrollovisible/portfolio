"VISION" — How One Poster Almost Broke Me

Posted By Bintang 21 June 2025

Not gonna lie, this piece went through like 6 versions before it clicked. Started with a rough sketch, threw in some grunge textures, and let the chaos speak for itself. Here's the messy (but honest) process behind it.

"The scariest part wasn't the imagery — it was staring at a blank canvas for two days straight, wondering if I'd lost the vision entirely."

I scrapped the first three drafts entirely — too clean, too "safe" for what I wanted this piece to feel like. The breakthrough came when I stopped trying to make it pretty and just let the textures do the talking: cracked glass, grainy noise, that unsettling eye motif staring right back at you. Sometimes the best design decisions come from letting go of control.
